Little shrines like this are everywhere in Mexico. Of course they've appeared lots in Colorado in the last couple of decades. But apparently everywhere somebody was killed in a car wreck you'll find a little shrine. Sometimes they're lavishly attended, sometimes they're built with concrete and sometimes they're quite large.

DSC02177 Old church in Zacan
Ladies fixing lunch for us at Paricutin volcano. They made hand-made tortillas, which are prized throughout the country. The vast majority of the corn tortillas are made by machines at the tortillerias in every town, but they end up looking like the ones we see in the store. The hand-made ones are thicker and crispier and really are delicious.
DSC02195
This is Fernando, riding up the road that climbs out of the Patzcuaro lake basin. We met a number of serious recreational and competitive cyclists (probably about 10 of them on our trip). They were riding well on fine equipment and all said they were training for races. Our friend Eli on the way up the Mil Cumbres road taught us a lot about riding with traffic. I'd like to be able to ride half as well as he does with traffic.
